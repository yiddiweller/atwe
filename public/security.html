How your messages are protected

Written to be read, not to reassure. Where Atwe is strong, it says so. Where it is not, it says that too — because a promise you cannot check is worth nothing.

The short version

Ordinary chats Private, encrypted in transit, but not end-to-end

Everything between your phone and Atwe travels over HTTPS, and your messages are stored on our servers so your history follows you to a new phone and to the web. That storage is the trade: it means Atwe could read an ordinary message, and so could anybody who compelled or breached us. We do not sell it, we do not advertise against it, and access is logged — but "we choose not to" is a policy, not a guarantee, and we will not dress it up as one.

Secret chats End-to-end encrypted, and we genuinely cannot read them

Turn on a secret chat with somebody and your two devices agree a key between themselves. Your half of it never leaves your phone — not in a backup, not in our database, nowhere we could hand it over. What reaches our servers is a scrambled blob, stored and delivered exactly as it arrived. This is not a policy. There is nothing to hand over.

What a secret chat costs you

These are real and we would rather you knew them before you needed them, not after:

  • Your history will not follow you to a new phone. The key is on the old one. That is the point of it.
  • Lose the device and you lose the conversation. There is no copy anywhere for us to restore.
  • Search will not find those messages, and a notification cannot preview them, because we cannot read them either.
  • One person at a time. Group chats are not end-to-end encrypted.

Check it yourself

Every secret chat shows a safety code — a short string of characters worked out from both of your keys. Read it aloud to each other, or compare it in person. If the two match, nobody is sitting in the middle. If they ever change without either of you reinstalling, stop and ask why.

What else protects an account

Passkeys

Sign in with your face, your fingerprint or your device PIN. There is no password to phish, and a passkey checks the site's real address, so a convincing fake gets nothing.

Two-factor authentication

An authenticator app, with single-use backup codes kept only as hashes.

Every session, revocable

See where you are signed in and end any of them. Changing your password ends all of them and cuts the live connections immediately.

Money moves once

Every payment path claims the work before it charges, so a double-tap, a retry or two servers racing cannot take money twice. A wallet cannot go negative — the database itself refuses.

Your data, on request

Download everything on your account at any time, and ask for it to be deleted. Requests are tracked against the legal deadline.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your messages, your contacts or your behaviour to anybody.
  • We do not read your chats to target advertising.
  • We do not claim end-to-end encryption on chats that are not end-to-end encrypted.

Found something wrong?

Tell us before you tell anybody else and we will fix it. Write to security@atwe.com with enough detail to reproduce it. We will not take legal action against anybody who reports a problem in good faith and gives us a reasonable chance to fix it.
